```
#StrokesAndVerses #StrokesAndVersesKinusaiga #ChandigarhWorkshops #TricityEvents #KinusaigaArt
```

The shape, every time:
1. **Hook** in capitals: the workshop name and one word of news (BACK, NEW, THIS SUNDAY).
2. **What it is** in one or two plain sentences.
3. **Why come**: the feeling, or what makes it special.
4. **Details block** with the fixed emoji, one fact per line, in this order: date, time, place, host, materials.
5. **How to book**: phone and a DM keyword in capitals (the keyword is the art form).
6. **Hashtags**: five to eight, at the end. `#StrokesAndVerses` first, the workshop tag second, then location tags.

## Replying to comments and DMs

- Reply to "details pls" within the day with a short line and the details block, then "Shall I save you a seat?"
- Thank every compliment by name: "Thank you, Neihal! See you at the next one 🌻"
- Keep firm lines friendly: "This one is for people who love slow, careful work, and we'd love to have you."
