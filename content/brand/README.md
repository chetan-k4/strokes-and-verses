Strokes & Verses is Balpreet Kaur's art studio in Sector 37-B, Chandigarh: workshops (Kinusaiga, pearl art, texture art, embossing, paper collage, denim upcycling), art therapy sessions, commissioned paintings, and hand-painted utility articles and apparel. The studio also runs sessions in Panchkula and Mumbai.

The brand is built from what is already on her wall: the pink neon sign, a ring with "Strokes & Verses" written inside it in a looping hand. The kit turns that sign into the logo, keeps the blush pink and flowers of the original mark, and adds two working typefaces so every post, poster and tag looks like it came from the same studio.

**Tagline:** Where art meets heart.
**Promise:** A room where anyone, with no training, makes something they are proud of, and leaves calmer than they came in.

## Name and naming

- Write the name as **Strokes & Verses** everywhere: headlines, captions, signage. The ampersand is part of the name.
- The place is **Strokes & Verses Studio, Sector 37-B, Chandigarh**. Retire "Strokes Shop", "Strokes and Verses Art Studio" and "colour strokes and verses".
- The founder is **Balpreet** in captions and **Balpreet Kaur, Founder** on cards and credits.
- Workshops are named **[Art form] Workshop**: "Kinusaiga Workshop", "Pearl Art Workshop". Capitalise the art form. Spell Kinusaiga as one word, capital K only.
- The one hashtag that is always used is `#StrokesAndVerses`. Workshop tags follow `#StrokesAndVerses` + art form: `#StrokesAndVersesKinusaiga`.

## Voice

Balpreet's captions already have a voice: warm, grateful, excited to share something new, and generous with credit to participants. Keep that and make it tidier.

- **Warm and first-person plural.** "We're bringing Kinusaiga back." Balpreet speaks as "I" only in personal notes and gratitude reels.
- **Show the feeling, then the facts.** Lead with what it feels like ("Slow, meditative and super satisfying"), follow with the details block.
- **Beginner-first.** Every workshop post says who it is for and that materials are included. Nobody needs to be "an artist" to come.
- **Gratitude is a ritual.** After every workshop, a thank-you post names the art form, thanks participants and collaborators by handle, and teases what's next.
- **Hinglish is welcome** in reels and stories (her "बार फिर बात की जाए, आपसे" works). Keep captions' details block in English so it can be searched.
- **Emoji:** the signature pair is ✨ and 🌻. Use at most three decorative emoji per caption. The details block uses a fixed set of functional emoji (see the Voice & captions section).
- **Avoid:** strings of hashtags pasted into the body, asterisks for bold (they show literally on Instagram), ALL CAPS sentences, "DM fr" shorthand in the bio.

## Visual foundations

- **Colour.** Two themes. `studio` (cream `canvas`, `ink` text) is the default for everything posted in daylight. `neon-night` (`canvas` near-black, `neon` lit pink) is for launches, reel covers, evening events and anything that should feel like the sign switched on. `neon` is the one brand colour for logo, buttons, links and a single highlighted word. `blush` is the heritage fill for avatars, tags and poster panels; put `on-blush` text on it, never `neon`. `sunflower` is a small accent for stickers and "new" flags, always with `on-sunflower` text. Keep to canvas + ink + neon + one of blush or sunflower in any single layout.
- **Type.** Three families, each with one job. `script` (Sacramento) is the neon hand: the logo, a sign-off, a single word of feeling, at 40px or larger. `serif` (Fraunces) carries headlines (`display`, `headline`) and the italic standfirst (`title-italic`). `sans` (Bricolage Grotesque) carries body text, facts and the `poster` style for workshop names in capitals. Never set a full sentence in script.
- **Shape.** Round things echo the ring: pill buttons (`radius-pill`), circular date stamps, circle photo crops. Cards use `radius-md`; big poster panels `radius-lg`.
- **Photography.** Her real studio is the best asset. Shoot participants' hands and finished pieces against the cream wall, the hexagon cork boards and the neon sign. Daylight, warm white balance, no heavy filters. Put the finished piece in the centre third and leave the top or bottom quarter clear for type.
- **Glow.** `shadow-glow` is the neon halo. Use it once per layout, on the logo ring or the primary button, in `neon-night` mostly.
- **Motion (reels).** Text appears in one soft fade or a left-to-right "light up" like a neon tube; no bouncing or spinning type.
- **Focus.** On the web, keyboard focus is a 2px solid `focus-ring` with 2px offset.

## Logo system

The full rules are in the Logo section. In short: the **primary mark** (`sv-mark-*`) is the neon ring with "Strokes / & / Verses" stacked inside and a five-petal blossom sitting in the ring's opening, the flower carried over from the original logo. The **wordmark** (`sv-wordmark-*`) is the one-line version with "STUDIO · BY BALPREET KAUR" beneath, for wide spaces. The **monogram** (`sv-monogram-*`) is S&V in the ring, for stamps, stickers, tags and favicons. The **blossom** (`sv-blossom-*`) is the motif alone.

## Iconography

- The blossom is the only brand icon. Use it as a bullet, a divider, a watermark or a sticker.
- For event details, use the fixed emoji set: 🗓️ date, ⏰ time, 📍 place, 👩‍🎨 host, 🎨 materials, 📞 booking. On printed pieces, set these facts as plain labels in `label` style instead of emoji.
- No clip-art palettes or paintbrush icons: her real work is the imagery.
