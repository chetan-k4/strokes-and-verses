# Logo

The logo is the studio's neon sign, redrawn as a clean line: a thin ring, "Strokes & Verses" in a monoline looping hand, and a five-petal blossom where the ring opens at the top right. The blossom comes from the flowering branch in the original logo, so the old and new marks stay related.

## The family

| Asset | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| `sv-mark-neon` | Default. Posters, flyers, website header, watermark on photos, on `canvas` or `paper` (Studio). |
| `sv-mark-ink` | One-colour printing: rubber stamps, screen printing, photocopies, engraving, on light grounds. |
| `sv-mark-cream` | On dark photos and on `neon-night` backgrounds. |
| `sv-avatar-blush` | Instagram, WhatsApp Business and Google profile picture. Blush disc, ink mark, same pink as the current avatar so followers recognise it. |
| `sv-avatar-night` | Alternative profile picture for launches and festive seasons: the sign switched on. |
| `sv-wordmark-*` | Wide, short spaces: email signature, website footer, banners, the bottom of a poster. Includes "STUDIO · BY BALPREET KAUR". |
| `sv-monogram-*` | Small sizes: stickers, hang tags, the corner of reels, favicon, stamps on packaging. |
| `sv-blossom-*` | The motif alone, as a bullet, divider or sticker. |

SVG files are for printers and designers; PNG files (transparent background) are for Canva, WhatsApp and Instagram.

## Rules

- **Clear space:** keep a margin around the ring equal to the height of the "&" on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** primary mark 96px (web) or 25 mm (print). Below that, use the monogram; its minimum is 32px or 10 mm.
- **Colour:** neon on light, cream on dark, ink for one-colour jobs. The mark is always a single colour.
- **On photos:** place the mark over a calm, light area (the cream wall) in neon, or over a dark area in cream. If neither exists, put the mark on a `paper` disc.
- **Don't:** stretch or squash it, rotate it, add outlines or drop shadows, recolour it in sunflower or blush, rearrange the three words, remove the blossom, or set the name in another script and call it the logo.
- **Glow:** in `neon-night` layouts the ring may take `shadow-glow` (a soft pink halo). Nowhere else.

## Migration from the old logo

1. Change the Instagram, WhatsApp and Google profile pictures to `sv-avatar-blush.png` on the same day.
2. Replace the old logo in the top-left of post templates with `sv-monogram-neon`.
3. Use up existing printed stock; reprint cards and tags with the new mark when they run out.
4. The neon sign stays exactly as it is: it is the logo.
